# GeerSoft IT Solutions — Enterprise AI & Digital Systems Engineering

Official web platform for GeerSoft IT Solutions, engineered with modern Enterprise AI design patterns, interactive cost estimation, dynamic multi-theme dock, and full mobile-first responsiveness.

## Architecture & Structure
- `demo/` — Core production application
  - `index.php` — Interactive AI Homepage with Male Cybernetic Backdrop & DEMOFIT metrics
  - `about.php` — Enterprise AI Engineering Team & Autonomous Lab Showcase
  - `services_all.php` & `service-detail.php` — Full-spectrum IT & Agentic AI solutions
  - `portfolio.php` & `portfolio-popup.php` — Production case studies (FinTech, SaaS, Healthcare AI, Logistics, Cloud)
  - `price.php` — Transparent AI project tiers & interactive budget estimator
  - `blogs.php` & `blog-details.php` — Technology insights & thought leadership
  - `contact.php` — Project inquiry & communication hub
  - `include/header.php` & `include/footer.php` — Universal navigation with 4-color dynamic theme switcher
  - `assets/` — Lightweight, optimized production assets (CSS, JS, WebFonts, Images)

## 4 Dynamic Theme Modes
- **Obsidian Cobalt Blue** (`cyber-blue`)
- **Slate Titanium Violet** (`quantum-violet`)
- **Emerald FinTech Green** (`matrix-emerald`)
- **Executive Bronze Orange** (`solar-amber`)
- **Dual View Modes:** Dark Cyber & Pristine Light View
